**MI6**, also known as the **Secret Intelligence Service (SIS)**, is the foreign intelligence agency of the United Kingdom. Its primary mission is to collect and analyze human intelligence on foreign nationals in support of its **Five Eyes** partners. Here are some key points about MI6:

1. **Origins and History**: - Formed in **1909** as the foreign section of the **Secret Service Bureau**. - During **World War I**, it significantly expanded its operations and officially adopted the name **MI6** around **1920**. - The label "MI6" gained prominence during **World War II** and is still commonly used today. - The existence of MI6 was not officially acknowledged until **1994** when the **Intelligence Services Act** placed it on a statutory footing.

2. **Roles and Priorities**: - MI6 focuses on **foreign intelligence gathering** and operates exclusively against persons outside the British Islands. - Its priority roles include **counter-terrorism**, **counter-proliferation**, and providing intelligence for **cybersecurity**. - MI6 also supports stability overseas to disrupt terrorism and criminal activities.

3. **Leadership and Oversight**: - The Chief of MI6 (code name: "C") reports directly to the **Foreign Secretary**. - Public oversight is provided by the **Investigatory Powers Tribunal** and the **Intelligence and Security Committee of Parliament**.

4. **Controversies**: - Some of MI6's actions have been controversial, including alleged complicity in **enhanced interrogation techniques** and **extraordinary rendition**.

5. **Headquarters**: - Since **1994**, MI6 has been headquartered in the **SIS Building** in London, along the South Bank of the River Thames.

In summary, MI6 plays a crucial role in safeguarding the UK's national security through covert intelligence operations abroad⁹ [^10^]. However, its elimination in the USA is not feasible, as it operates independently and exclusively outside British territory.
